# 精选示例与提示词

[English](selected-gallery.md)

以下五张由维护者选定。保留实际英文生成提示词，并附中文复用版；中文版本未单独生成验证。工作台原始提示词含暖白描述，属于严格纯白要求之前的记录，原文不作追改；新生成请使用纯白背景。

## 创作者的工作台

![创作者的工作台](../assets/examples/07-workspace.png)

用于开发者工具、个人主页或效率主题配图。

### 中文复用提示词

```text
为开发者工具或个人主页绘制创作者工作台：三分之四俯视角的小桌、空白屏幕笔记本电脑、台灯、口袋笔记本、铅笔、头戴耳机和稍拉出的椅子。器物完整、接触可信，四周留白。用干涩墨线、黑色块面、点描和短排线塑造形体，不加文字或标志。新生成时使用纯黑墨色与纯白背景。
```

### 实际英文生成提示词

```text
A beautifully composed black ink editorial illustration of a creative developer's small workspace, for a personal portfolio or developer-tool landing page. A compact desk seen from an inviting elevated three-quarter angle: an open unbranded laptop with completely blank light screen, a small articulated desk lamp bent toward a pocket notebook and pencil, over-ear headphones casually resting beside it, and a simple chair partially pulled out. A single cable curves naturally under the desk. No other props. Asymmetrical elegant composition, complete furniture visible, objects physically grounded, generous clean warm-white negative space surrounding the scene. Inkbit visual language: crisp slightly dry hand-inked contours, expressive strong black shapes on lamp shade and chair, hand stippling and short directional hatching to suggest depth, restrained midtone areas, no photorealistic texture or smooth gradients. Sophisticated vintage editorial print, lively but not cute clipart, not isometric vector art, not a glossy 3D render. Black ink only on near-white paper, no color or sepia, no writing, no letters, no code, no logos, no frame. Square illustration.
```

## 城市骑行

![城市骑行](../assets/examples/08-city-cycling.png)

用于城市指南、骑行路线或旅行专题。

### 中文复用提示词

```text
绘制城市骑行主题插画：自行车自然倚在河边矮石墙上，后车篮固定小帆布包，远处是桥和简化城镇。车轮落地、接触可信，上方留白供标题排版。纯黑墨色与纯白背景，用疏密点阵、干涩轮廓和短排线表现层次，不加文字。
```

### 实际英文生成提示词

```text
Create a beautiful practical editorial illustration for an urban cycling travel article. A city bicycle leaning naturally against a low stone wall beside a quiet riverside, a small canvas bag secured in the rear basket, a bridge and a few simplified town buildings far away. No people. Elegant asymmetrical landscape composition, ample empty white sky for separately typeset headline. STRICT ACHROMATIC BLACK INK ON PURE WHITE #FFFFFF background. All RGB channels equal; no cream, ivory, warm paper, sepia, yellow, brown or colored accents. No aged paper texture. Inkbit style: bold black silhouette accents, slightly dry expressive ink contours, irregular-density stippling and short form-following hatching. Shade by black marks separated by white gaps, no photographic grey wash or smooth gradients. Moderately simplified inviting shapes, not photographic engraving, not vector clipart, not glossy 3D. Coherent bicycle geometry with two circular wheels on ground, credible leaning contact. No text, logos, borders or watermark. Single finished illustration.
```

## 音乐聆听

![音乐聆听](../assets/examples/09-music.png)

用于音乐产品、专辑推荐或音频专题。

### 中文复用提示词

```text
绘制音乐聆听主题插画：低柜上的唱片机、从空白封套中露出的黑胶唱片，以及自然放在柜角的头戴耳机和松弛线缆。三分之四视角、留白充足，以黑色唱片和耳机平衡白色平面。纯黑墨色与纯白背景，用点描和短排线塑形，不加文字、标志或音符装饰。
```

### 实际英文生成提示词

```text
Create an appealing Inkbit editorial illustration for a music listening app or album discovery article: a compact vinyl turntable on a low cabinet, one large record partially sliding from a blank sleeve leaning against the cabinet, and over-ear headphones resting naturally on its corner with their cable making one loose curve. Simple refined domestic music corner, no room background and no extra objects. Three-quarter view, coherent perspective, clear silhouettes, asymmetrical arrangement with generous pure white margins. STRICT neutral BLACK #000000 INK ON PURE WHITE #FFFFFF. No ivory, cream, warm paper, yellow aging, brown, sepia or other colors. No background texture. Black solid record and headset accents balanced with white planes. Shade using irregular black stippling and short dry ink hatch strokes, not gradients or photographic grey rendering. Distinct hand-drawn expressive contours, inviting modest simplification, sophisticated printlike editorial art, not technical diagram, not vector clipart, not 3D. No text, fake lettering, logos, musical note decorations, borders or watermark. Square standalone illustration.
```

## 烘焙厨房

![烘焙厨房](../assets/examples/10-baking.png)

用于食谱、烘焙课程或厨房工具介绍。

### 中文复用提示词

```text
绘制烘焙主题插画：台面上放着内置打蛋器的搅拌碗、斜放的擀面杖、布上的面团和两颗鸡蛋，后方挂一只烤箱手套。略俯视的三分之四角度，器物完整，接触与椭圆透视可信。黑碗白边，以短排线表现木头与布料，以点阵表现阴影。纯黑墨色与纯白背景，不加文字。
```

### 实际英文生成提示词

```text
A refined Inkbit black ink editorial illustration for a baking recipe collection: a mixing bowl with a wire whisk resting inside on a kitchen counter, a rolling pin at a diagonal, a small ball of dough and two eggs on a folded plain cloth. A simple oven mitt hangs on a hook behind. No people or hands. Beautiful readable arrangement with generous white margins, whole main objects visible, plausible ellipses and physical contacts, gently elevated three-quarter view. Black bowl exterior with white rim and white highlights, short hatching describes wood and cloth, sparse irregular dots describe flour and shadows. Confident slightly dry hand-drawn contours, appealing simplified shapes with selective detail. PURE BLACK INK ON PURE WHITE #FFFFFF, no cream paper, no brown or sepia, no yellowing, no colored accents, no photographic gray wash, no paper grain overlay. No text, labels, brands, border, watermark, or glossy 3D. Square finished illustration.
```

## 湖畔露营

![湖畔露营](../assets/examples/11-camping.png)

用于户外旅行、露营计划或夜景主题。

### 中文复用提示词

```text
绘制湖畔露营夜景：小帐篷搭在高山湖旁，前景平石上放露营灯，远处松林和山脊上方是星空。帐篷绳索固定到地面，不画人物与篝火。黑色块面与白色星点、水面倒影形成对比，用干涩轮廓、疏密点阵和短排线表现纵深。严格黑白，不加彩色灯光、文字或边框。
```

### 实际英文生成提示词

```text
Create a black and white Inkbit editorial outdoor illustration: a small pitched tent beside a still alpine lake, a camping lantern standing on a flat stone in the foreground, distant pine silhouettes and mountain ridges beneath a large starry sky. No people. Quiet night scene expressed in black ink masses with pure white stars and white lake reflections, no gradients. Clearly drawn tent ropes anchored to ground, no campfire, no clutter. Landscape composition with layered depth, expressive slightly dry ink contours, irregular hand stippling and short directional hatching. STRICTLY BLACK AND WHITE, pure white unprinted areas, no cream or tinted paper, no sepia brown, no blue, no colored light, no aged background texture. Illustrative and graphic not photorealistic. No text, symbols, border or watermark.
```
